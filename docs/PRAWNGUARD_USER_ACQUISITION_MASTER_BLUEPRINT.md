# PrawnGuard.ai: Master 20-Strategy User Acquisition Blueprint
## Hyper-Local Grassroots, AI Video Engine, Product-Led Growth & Digital Community Flywheel for Coastal Andhra Pradesh

---

### Executive Overview & Strategic Moat
PrawnGuard.ai is engineered as an offline-first aquaculture AI intelligence & finance mobile platform (Flutter + Supabase + Gemini Flash + On-Device Vision) specifically customized for commercial Vannamei shrimp farmers in Andhra Pradesh (Bhimavaram, Akividu, Kaikaluru, Nellore, Kavali, Machilipatnam, Bapatla, Kakinada). 

Shrimp aquaculture is a high-risk, high-capital enterprise (₹6L–₹8L/acre per 100-day cycle). Farmers operate in muddy, low-connectivity coastal belts, battle sudden disease outbreaks (WSSV, EHP, White Gut, Vibrio), suffer predatory middleman deductions at harvest weighbridges, and face catastrophic midnight hypoxia (DO crashes). They do not trust generic ads, English tech jargon, or unverified claims.

This Master Blueprint synthesizes 20 battle-tested, hyper-localized acquisition strategies across 4 distinct categories:
- **Category A:** Grassroots & Aquaculture Ecosystem Channels (6 Strategies)
- **Category B:** AI-Powered Video Generation & Content Machine (6 Strategies with explicit toolchains, scripts, and workflows)
- **Category C:** Product-Led Growth, WhatsApp Loops & Offline Virality (5 Strategies)
- **Category D:** Hyper-Local Digital Marketing & Community Flywheels (3 Strategies)

### Strategy A1: "Lab-in-Pocket" Co-Branded Diagnostic Engine at Local Water Testing Labs
- **Target Farmer/Partner Persona:** Private Aqua Water Quality & PCR Labs (~400+ labs clustered across Bhimavaram, Akividu, Kaikaluru, Kavali, Nellore, and Machilipatnam) and visiting farmers bringing morning 500ml pond water samples.
- **Ground Context:** Between 6:00 AM and 9:30 AM daily, 30–70 farmers visit each local lab. Lab technicians are the highest-trust daily advisors, but currently handwrite parameters on flimsy carbon paper slips that smudge and get lost.
- **Step-by-Step Tactical Execution Playbook:**
  1. *Deploy Lab Web Portal:* Equip lab technicians with a lightweight, 30-second digital entry tool (PrawnGuard Lab Portal) accessible on any mobile browser, tablet, or desktop.
  2. *Capture 6 Core Parameters:* Technician logs pH, Dissolved Oxygen (DO), Salinity (ppt), Total Alkalinity, Total Ammonia ($NH_3/NH_4^+$), and Vibrio yellow/green CFU plate counts.
  3. *Co-Branded WhatsApp Delivery:* System automatically sends an instant SMS/WhatsApp PDF report branded: *"Sri Balaji Aqua Lab & PrawnGuard.ai Joint Water Health Certificate"*.
  4. *Offline App Sync Hook:* The digital card features a 1-tap deep link & QR: *"Tap to load today's water reading into PrawnGuard.ai for 7-day predictive Hypoxia & Toxicity warnings"*.
  5. *Immediate Remediation Hook:* First-time app sync delivers instant AI Bio-Energetic chemical balancing (exact Agricultural Lime, Dolomite, or Zeolite kg/acre required for current pond depth), eliminating expensive chemical over-purchases.
- **Tech/Tools Required:** Flutter Web / Supabase DB, WhatsApp Cloud API, automated PDF generation service, Razorpay/Cashfree UPI payout gateway for technician bounties.
- **Estimated Budget / Cost per Install:**
  - Acrylic QR counter standee + branded Telugu writing mat: ₹350 per lab.
  - Technician bounty: ₹25 per active verified farmer install.
  - Blended CAC: ₹35 per high-intent active pond owner.
- **Expected Impact / Conversion KPI:**
