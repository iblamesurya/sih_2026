1. Executive Summary & Google Solo Developer Policy

On November 13, 2023, Google implemented strict publishing requirements for new personal/individual developer accounts. Solo developers face three core gates:

  • Government ID & International Card: Mandatory identity verification matching payment credentials ($25 USD one-time fee).
  • Mandatory 20-Tester 14-Day Closed Beta: Personal accounts cannot publish to production until at least 20 testers remain continuously opted into a closed test track for 14 continuous days.
  • Reviewer Credentials & Data Safety: Automated and manual reviewers in the US/EU require demo bypass credentials and exact Data Safety declarations for sensitive hardware access (Camera, Microphone, Location).

Step 1: Setting Up the Google Play Console Account

1.1 Personal vs. Organization Account

When registering at play.google.com/console/signup, select Personal Account.

  • Pros: No D-U-N-S (Dun & Bradstreet) number, no business incorporation certificates (CIN/GSTIN), no corporate bank utility bills required.
  • Obligation: Must pass the 20-tester closed beta track (full strategy below).

Step 2: Cryptographic Keystore & Signed AAB Build

2.1 Keystore Generation (RSA 2048-bit)

Run the automated script in project root or execute via JDK keytool:

# Using PowerShell script:
.\scripts\generate_keystore.ps1

# Or manual command:
keytool -genkeypair -v `
  -keystore android/app/upload-keystore.jks `
  -alias upload `
  -keyalg RSA `
  -keysize 2048 `
  -validity 10000 `
  -storepass PrawnGuardProd2026 `
  -keypass PrawnGuardProd2026 `
  -dname "CN=PrawnGuard Developer, OU=Mobile, O=AquaVerse, L=Bhimavaram, ST=Andhra Pradesh, C=IN"
CRITICAL WARNING: Never commit upload-keystore.jks or key.properties to GitHub. If you lose this keystore, you will be permanently locked out of pushing app updates to Google Play!

2.2 Compiling the Optimized Production AAB

Build the release bundle with full R8 tree-shaking, code minification, and symbol splitting:

flutter build appbundle --release --obfuscate --split-debug-info=build/app/outputs/symbols

Expected output location: build/app/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab (approx. 20-25 MB).

Step 4: The 20-Tester 14-Day Closed Beta Playbook

The Golden Rule: Recruit 25 to 30 testers so that even if several uninstall or turn off their devices, your continuous count never drops below 20!

4.1 Tester Management via Google Group

  1. Create a group at groups.google.com: prawnguard-testers@googlegroups.com.
  2. Set permissions to "Anyone can join".
  3. In Play Console: Testing > Closed testing > Testers > Google Groups, add prawnguard-testers@googlegroups.com.
  4. Testers join the group once and immediately get web & Play Store opt-in access.
