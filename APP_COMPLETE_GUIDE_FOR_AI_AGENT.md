## 2. Target Audience Profile & Core Pain Points (Why Farmers Download)

1. **The ₹8 Lakh/Acre Overnight Disease Wipeout:**
   - Investing in 1 acre of shrimp farming costs ₹6,00,000 to ₹8,00,000 per crop cycle (90–120 days).
   - Diseases like **White Spot (WSSV - తెల్లమచ్చ)**, **White Gut (WGD - తెల్ల రెట్ట)**, **EHP (పెరుగుదల ఆగిపోవడం)**, and **Running Mortality (RMS)** can wipe out an entire pond in 48 hours.
   - Traditional water/shrimp testing labs are far away in town and take 2–3 days for results—by then, the crop is dead.
2. **The 60% Feed Cost Bleed (FCR Problem):**
   - Feed accounts for **60%–70%** of total farming cost (1 ton of feed costs ~₹95,000).
   - Workers throw feed by rough guesswork. Overfeeding by just 15 kg/day wastes **₹1,50,000 per crop** and rots at the pond bottom, spiking toxic **Ammonia (NH3)** and **Hydrogen Sulfide (H2S)**.
3. **The 3:00 AM Dissolved Oxygen (DO) Crash:**
   - Dissolved Oxygen drops critically between 2:00 AM and 5:00 AM, especially during cloudy weather or sudden rain. If aerators aren't turned on or peroxide isn't added immediately, thousands of prawns float dead by sunrise.
4. **Zero Network Signal & Illiteracy / Typing Barrier at Ponds:**
   - Remote ponds have poor cellular reception. Paper diaries get wet in pond water, and older farmers or pond workers struggle to type complex forms in English.
5. **The Middleman / Trader Harvest Price Trap:**
   - On harvest day, local middlemen lie about current export market rates for 30-count, 40-count, or 50-count shrimp. Losing just ₹20–₹25 per kg on a 5-ton harvest steals **₹1,00,000 to ₹1,25,000** of pure profit from the farmer in a single day.

## 3. Complete Feature-by-Feature Breakdown (10 Core Modules)

### Feature 1: PrawnDoc AI — 5-Second Camera Disease Scanner
- **What It Does:** Farmers take a photo of a sick or weak shrimp using their smartphone camera. The multimodal AI vision engine scans the shrimp and identifies **12+ major shrimp diseases** in under 5 seconds.
- **Diseases Detected:**
  1. White Spot Syndrome Virus (WSSV - తెల్లమచ్చ వ్యాధి)
  2. White Gut Disease / White Feces Syndrome (WGD / WFS - తెల్ల రెట్ట వ్యాధి)
  3. Enterocytozoon hepatopenaei (EHP - రొయ్య పెరుగుదల ఆగిపోవడం / సైజు వేరియేషన్)
  4. Running Mortality Syndrome (RMS - రోజువారీ రొయ్యలు చనిపోవడం)
  5. Black Gill Disease (నల్ల మొప్పల వ్యాధి)
  6. Loose Shell Syndrome (గుల్ల మెత్తబడటం)
  7. Vibriosis (విబ్రియో బాక్టీరియా)
  8. Tail Rot / Fin Rot (తోక కుళ్ళు వ్యాధి)
  9. Cramped Tail / Muscle Necrosis (తోక వంకర / కండరాల తెలుపు)
  10. Mineral Deficiency & Molting Issues (గుల్ల విడిచే సమస్యలు)
- **Smart RAG Context:** Unlike generic image scanners, PrawnDoc AI automatically combines the photo with the pond's latest water parameters (`pH`, `DO`, `Ammonia`, `Salinity`) to give an exact **Severity Rating**, **Confidence %**, **Immediate Medicine/Probiotic Dosage**, and **Preventive Steps in Telugu**.
- **Marketing Story Hook:** *"Don't wait 3 days for a lab report while your pond dies—scan daily in 5 seconds with your phone camera."*

### Feature 2: Bio-Energetic Feed AI — Smart 4-Meal Feed Calculator
- **What It Does:** Eliminates blind feed guessing. Calculates the exact kilograms of feed required per day and splits it across the **4 standard aquaculture feeding times**:
  - **6:00 AM:** 20% of daily ration
  - **11:00 AM:** 30% of daily ration
  - **4:00 PM:** 30% of daily ration
  - **9:00 PM:** 20% of daily ration
- **How the AI Calculates It:**
  - Uses total stocked post-larvae (PL count), Days of Culture (DOC), Survival Rate %, and Average Body Weight (ABW in grams) to compute live pond biomass.
  - Adjusts automatically for **Water Temperature** (reduces feed when too cold or dangerously hot).
  - **Check-Tray Feedback Loop:** After each meal, the farmer selects how much feed was left in the check-tray (Empty / 10% left / 25% left / >50% left), and Feed AI automatically adjusts the next meal by **-20% to +10%**.
- **Marketing Story Hook:** *"Stop dumping ₹1.5 Lakhs of expensive feed into the pond mud. Feed AI tells you the exact kg for every single meal."*

### Feature 3: Telugu Voice NLU — Hands-Free Voice Pond Logging
- **What It Does:** Farmers don't need to type a single letter. They tap the microphone icon and speak naturally in Telugu (or mixed Telugu-English).
- **Example Spoken Input:**  
  *"చెరువు ఒకటిలో పీహెచ్ 7.9 ఉంది, ఆక్సిజన్ 4.8, అమ్మోనియా సున్నా, మేత 30 కేజీలు వేసాను"*  
  *(In Pond 1, pH is 7.9, Oxygen is 4.8, Ammonia is 0, fed 30 kg).*
- **How It Works:** The built-in Telugu Natural Language Understanding (NLU) engine automatically parses speech into structured telemetry fields:
  - Dissolved Oxygen (`DO` in ppm)
  - `pH` level
  - Salinity (`ppt`)
  - Ammonia (`NH3` in ppm)
  - Alkalinity (`ppm`)
  - Water Temperature (`°C`)
  - Feed Quantity (`kg`)
- **Marketing Story Hook:** *"Wet hands at the pond? Don't know English typing? Just speak in Telugu and the app writes your pond diary automatically!"*

### Feature 5: 100% Offline-First Rural Sync Engine
- **What It Does:** Guarantees zero data loss and smooth app usage even in remote coastal villages with **0 bars of cellular network ("No Service")**.

## 5. High-Conversion 15-Second Story Formula for AI Video Agents

Whenever generating a **15-second promotional video** for this app, follow this strict **3-Beat Two-Farmer Story Structure**:

1. **Seconds 00:00 – 00:03 (The Relatable Hook):**  
   Farmer B walks up to Farmer A on the pond bund and asks why Farmer A looks stressed, sad, angry, or exhausted.
2. **Seconds 00:03 – 00:07 (The Specific Pain Point):**  
   Farmer A states ONE specific aquaculture nightmare with a real rupee number (e.g., *"White Gut hit my ₹5 Lakh pond"*, *"Wasted ₹1 Lakh on excess feed"*, *"Didn't sleep all night watching aerators"*, *"Trader cheated me by ₹25/kg"*).
3. **Seconds 00:07 – 00:15 (The App Solution + CTA):**  
   Farmer B pulls out his smartphone, demonstrates the exact **AquaVerse / PrawnGuard** feature that solves that exact problem in seconds, and tells Farmer A (and the viewer) to download the app for free right now.
